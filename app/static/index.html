Procurement Audit Planner

West Berkshire Council · agentic audit planning pipeline

How to use this demo

What this is. An agentic system that plans an internal audit of procurement for West Berkshire Council from its public data. Six stages turn the Contract Procedure Rules, a year of spend and past audit papers into a risk register, an auditor decision gate, and a cross-checked audit planning pack. The agents and skills here are the same files that ran in Claude Code.

Browsing is free. You are looking at a complete committed run. Click any of the six stages to read its outputs. Nothing you do while browsing calls a model or costs anything.

Try it live (access code needed). Enter the access code above, then open stage 4, change one decision (for example make R-02 an OVERRIDE with likelihood=2), and submit. The risk-assessor agent reruns live, streams its progress here, and the revised register appears in the Live run view. Each live run costs real money and is capped; only one runs at a time. Stage 6 lets you record a sign-off. Committed outputs are never changed.

Outputs are risk indicators to investigate, not findings of wrongdoing by the council, any department or any supplier.
